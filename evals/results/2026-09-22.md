## Trust tier re-scored after judge calibration

The trust numbers above came from a judge that had not been calibrated. Stella hand-scored all 11 trust
questions the same day (`evals/judge_calibration.md`): the judge agreed on 9 of 11, and both misses passed an
answer whose substance was in the rows rather than in anything the answer said. The judge prompt and two rubrics
were tightened to match the human verdicts, and trust questions no longer fail on route or metric expectations,
which belong to the path tier.

Re-run with the tightened judge (`20260922T134927.jsonl`): **5 of 11 (45.5%)**, against 7 of 11 before. No
change was made to the agent between the two runs, so the movement is the ruler, not the thing being measured.
The corrected figure is the baseline for the work on business rules and the answer composer.

## Answer composer, same day

`agent/compose.py` landed after the run above: every answer now states its finding first, computed from the rows,
then definition, scope, period and caveats. The trust tier was re-run on its 11 questions
(`20260922T17xxxx` series): **8 of 11 (72.7%)**, against 5 of 11 with the calibrated judge and no composer.

Two regressions the full run caught, both the composer's own fault and both fixed:

- "Why did people leave" was answered with "comp has the highest share at 33%, across 6 groups", hiding four of
  the six recorded reasons. Breakdowns of up to eight groups are now read out in full.
- A generated-SQL answer was ranked by a `year` column, producing "performance has the highest year at 2,024".
  Generated SQL now refuses to rank by anything that reads as a label, an identifier or a calendar part, and says
  so instead of inventing a ranking when no column is obviously a measure.

The three trust failures that remain need context no number carries: the February 2023 restructuring, the reorg
behind the drop under njones, and internal applicants in the funnel. They are the baseline for the business-rule
work, not for another run of the same agent.
