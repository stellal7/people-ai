# Compensation Policy

**COMP-1.1** This policy covers pay bands, how pay is set and changed, and how pay is discussed. It does not cover individual pay, which is confidential and governed by the access rules in the data platform.

## COMP-2 Bands

**COMP-2.1** Every job has a pay band for each location. A band has a minimum, a midpoint and a maximum. The minimum is 80% of the midpoint and the maximum is 120% of the midpoint.

**COMP-2.2** Bands are set once a year and apply for the calendar year. Midpoints normally move up by about 3.5% at the start of the year.

**COMP-2.3** Location affects the band through a cost-of-living factor. Austin is the reference at 1.0; Bangalore sits well below it and London above.

**COMP-2.4** In January 2024 an additional market adjustment was applied to two families where hiring had become difficult: Data midpoints rose by a further 12% and Engineering by a further 6%. Salaries were not automatically raised to match, which moved many people lower in their band.

## COMP-3 Compa-ratio

**COMP-3.1** Compa-ratio is base salary divided by the midpoint of the band for that person's job and location, using the band in force on the date being measured.

**COMP-3.2** A compa-ratio below 0.90 is the trigger for a pay review conversation between the manager and their HR business partner.

**COMP-3.3** Because bands move at the start of each year, a compa-ratio can fall without any change in pay. Compare compa-ratios within a year before concluding that someone has fallen behind.

## COMP-4 Pay changes

**COMP-4.1** Merit increases take effect on 1 March each year and are based on the most recent rating: no increase at rating 1, about 1% at rating 2, about 3% at rating 3, about 4.5% at rating 4, and about 6% at rating 5.

**COMP-4.2** Promotion increases are 8% to 12% and are separate from merit.

**COMP-4.3** A move to another location changes the band, and pay is adjusted to hold the person's position in their new band.

**COMP-4.4** Bonus targets are a percentage of base: 5% at levels 3 and 4, 10% at levels 5 and 6, 15% at levels 7 and 8, 20% at level 9. Sales roles carry an additional 25% variable target.

**COMP-4.5** Equity is granted at hire and on promotion, and scales with level.

## COMP-5 Confidentiality

**COMP-5.1** Individual pay is visible to the person, their manager, and the HR business partner for their organisation. Everyone else sees aggregates only.

**COMP-5.2** Pay aggregates are not published for groups of fewer than five people.
