# Performance Management

**PERF-1.1** This policy covers ratings, support for people who are not meeting expectations, exits, and the engagement survey.

## PERF-2 Ratings

**PERF-2.1** Ratings are set on 30 June and 31 December. Someone must have at least 90 days of tenure on the rating date to be rated.

**PERF-2.2** A rating of 3 means fully meeting the expectations of the level. Ratings of 1 and 2 mean expectations are not being met.

**PERF-2.3** A rating is visible to the person, their manager, and the HR business partner for their organisation.

## PERF-3 When expectations are not met

**PERF-3.1** A rating of 1 or 2 starts a documented support plan agreed between the manager and the HR business partner.

**PERF-3.2** Managers are supported the same way as anyone else. A manager whose team shows sustained high voluntary attrition and low manager scores is expected to work with their HR business partner on a plan.

**PERF-3.3** An exit for performance follows a support plan that has not succeeded. It is recorded as an involuntary termination with reason `performance`.

## PERF-4 Exits

**PERF-4.1** Exits are either voluntary, meaning the person chose to leave, or involuntary, meaning the company did. Involuntary reasons are performance, misconduct, restructuring, and reduction in force.

**PERF-4.2** A voluntary exit is **regretted** when the person's last rating was 4 or 5. Regretted attrition is the number that matters for retention work; total attrition alone hides whether the company is losing the people it wants to keep.

**PERF-4.3** The termination date is the first day the person is no longer employed. Their last working day is the day before.

**PERF-4.4** Someone who leaves voluntarily with a last rating of 3 or higher is eligible for rehire.

## PERF-5 Engagement survey

**PERF-5.1** The engagement survey runs once a year, in October. Responses are confidential.

**PERF-5.2** Survey results are **never reported for groups of fewer than five respondents**. This holds for every role, including HR and executives: a manager cannot see their team's score if fewer than five people answered.

**PERF-5.3** The survey reports three scores: overall engagement, how people rate their manager, and career growth. Each runs from 1 to 5.

**PERF-5.4** Survey scores describe the people who answered, not the whole team. Response rates run around 70 to 75%.
