# Leave Policy (United Kingdom)

**LVE-1.1** This policy applies to employees based in the United Kingdom. Employees based in the United States follow the United States version of this policy.

## LVE-2 Annual leave

**LVE-2.1** Employees receive **25 days** of annual leave per year, in addition to bank holidays.

**LVE-2.2** Up to five unused days carry into the following year and must be used by 31 March.

## LVE-3 Family leave

**LVE-3.1** Statutory maternity, paternity, adoption and shared parental leave apply. Acme enhances pay to full salary for the **first 26 weeks** of maternity or primary adopter leave, and for **12 weeks** of paternity or secondary adopter leave.

## LVE-4 Leave of absence

**LVE-4.1** A leave of absence typically runs from one to five months and is agreed with the manager and the HR business partner before it starts.

**LVE-4.2** Someone on leave remains an employee. They are recorded with status `leave` and return with status `active`.

**LVE-4.3** People on leave are **not counted in headcount** by default, because headcount measures active employees.

**LVE-4.4** Time on leave does not count toward the twelve months in level required for promotion.

## LVE-5 Sickness

**LVE-5.1** Statutory sick pay applies from the fourth consecutive day of absence, enhanced to full pay for up to 13 weeks in any twelve-month period.

## LVE-6 Employee data

**LVE-6.1** Employee data for United Kingdom staff is processed under UK GDPR. Requests for people analytics that identify individuals require a lawful basis recorded by the HR business partner, and aggregate reporting is the default.
