# Promotion and Calibration

**PRO-1.1** This policy covers who may be promoted, when promotions take effect, and how performance ratings are calibrated.

## PRO-2 Cycles

**PRO-2.1** Promotions are decided twice a year and take effect on 15 March and 15 September.

**PRO-2.2** Performance ratings are set twice a year, on 30 June and 31 December, for the half year then ending.

**PRO-2.3** Merit pay increases take effect once a year, on 1 March, and are separate from promotion increases.

## PRO-3 Eligibility

**PRO-3.1** To be considered in a cycle, an individual contributor must have been at their current level for at least **twelve months** on the effective date of the cycle. Before 1 January 2024 this requirement was eighteen months.

**PRO-3.2** The person must be active on the effective date. People on leave are considered in the next cycle after their return.

**PRO-3.3** A rating of 3 or higher in the most recent cycle is required. A rating of 1 or 2 makes someone ineligible for that cycle.

**PRO-3.4** Level 7 individual contributors and level 9 managers are at the top of their track and are not considered for level promotion.

**PRO-3.5** Promotion into a people management role happens through succession or span growth, not through this cycle, and is described in the Leveling Guide.

## PRO-4 Ratings

**PRO-4.1** Ratings run from 1 to 5, where 3 means fully meeting the expectations of the level.

**PRO-4.2** Calibration compares people at the same level across teams before ratings are final. Expect roughly this shape company-wide: about 3% at 1, 12% at 2, 55% at 3, 22% at 4, and 8% at 5. This is a guide for calibration discussion, not a quota to force onto any one team.

**PRO-4.3** Every rating in this dataset is calibrated; uncalibrated ratings are not published.

## PRO-5 Pay on promotion

**PRO-5.1** A promotion carries an increase of 8% to 12%, and the new salary must be at least the minimum of the new band.

**PRO-5.2** A track change between equivalent levels carries no automatic increase.

## PRO-6 Records

**PRO-6.1** A promotion is recorded as a promotion event effective on the cycle date, with a matching pay record on the same date.

**PRO-6.2** Promotion rate is measured as promotions in a period over average eligible headcount, annualised. The definition of eligible lives with the metric, not in this policy.
