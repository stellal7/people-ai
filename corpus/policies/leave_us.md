# Leave Policy (United States)

**LVE-1.1** This policy applies to employees based in the United States. Employees based in the United Kingdom follow the United Kingdom version of this policy.

## LVE-2 Paid time off

**LVE-2.1** Employees receive **20 days** of paid time off per year, in addition to public holidays.

**LVE-2.2** Up to five unused days carry into the following year and must be used by 31 March.

## LVE-3 Parental leave

**LVE-3.1** Parents receive **12 weeks** of paid parental leave, available to either parent, within twelve months of birth or placement.

## LVE-4 Leave of absence

**LVE-4.1** A leave of absence typically runs from one to five months and is agreed with the manager and the HR business partner before it starts.

**LVE-4.2** Someone on leave remains an employee. They are recorded with status `leave` and return with status `active`.

**LVE-4.3** People on leave are **not counted in headcount** by default, because headcount measures active employees. Reports that need them say so explicitly.

**LVE-4.4** Time on leave does not count toward the twelve months in level required for promotion, and someone on leave during a promotion cycle is considered in the next cycle after their return.

**LVE-4.5** A person on leave is not counted as a direct report for span of control, so a manager's span dips while a report is away.

## LVE-5 Sick leave

**LVE-5.1** Sick leave is not deducted from paid time off. Absences beyond ten consecutive working days move to a leave of absence.
