# Leveling Guide (version 1, superseded)

**LEV-1.1** This guide defined the levels used across Acme Corp for decisions made between 1 January 2021 and 31 December 2023. It was replaced by version 2 on 1 January 2024. Decisions taken while this version was in force were made under these rules.

## LEV-2 Individual contributor track

**LEV-2.1** The individual contributor track ran from level 3 to level 6. Titles were: level 3 Associate, level 4 the base title for the family, level 5 Senior, level 6 Staff.

**LEV-2.2** Level 6 Staff was the highest individual contributor level. The Principal level (level 7) did not exist; engineers seeking scope beyond Staff moved to the manager track.

## LEV-3 Manager track

**LEV-3.1** The manager track ran from level 6 to level 9, with the same titles as today: Manager, Senior Manager, Director, VP.

## LEV-4 Span and layers

**LEV-4.1** A line manager was expected to support up to eight direct reports before a team was split.

## LEV-5 Leveling decisions

**LEV-5.1** Levels were set at hire and changed only through the promotion process.

**LEV-5.2** Promotion eligibility required **eighteen months in level**. This requirement was reduced to twelve months in version 2, effective 1 January 2024.
