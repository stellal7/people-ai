# Leveling Guide (version 2)

**LEV-1.1** This guide defines the levels used across Acme Corp, the expectations at each level, and how levels map to job titles. It replaces version 1, which applied to decisions made before 1 January 2024.

**LEV-1.2** Levels describe scope of work, not tenure. Time in role never creates an entitlement to the next level.

## LEV-2 Individual contributor track

**LEV-2.1** The individual contributor track runs from level 3 to level 7. Titles are: level 3 Associate, level 4 the base title for the family (for example Software Engineer), level 5 Senior, level 6 Staff, level 7 Principal.

**LEV-2.2** Level 3 delivers defined tasks with guidance. Level 4 owns features end to end. Level 5 owns a problem area and mentors others. Level 6 sets technical direction for a team and takes on work whose shape is unclear. Level 7 sets direction across several teams and is accountable for outcomes beyond their own team.

**LEV-2.3** Level 7 is the highest individual contributor level. Growth beyond level 7 means moving to the manager track, and is not automatic.

## LEV-3 Manager track

**LEV-3.1** The manager track runs from level 6 to level 9. Titles are: level 6 Manager, level 7 Senior Manager, level 8 Director, level 9 VP.

**LEV-3.2** A level 6 Manager leads one team. A level 7 Senior Manager leads a team through line managers. A level 8 Director leads several teams. A level 9 VP leads a function or division.

**LEV-3.3** Levels 6 and 7 exist on both tracks and are equivalent in scope and pay band. Moving from Staff engineer to Manager is a track change, not a promotion, and carries no automatic pay increase.

**LEV-3.4** Becoming a people manager happens in one of two ways: succession, when a manager leaves and a member of their team takes over, or span growth, when a team grows past the point one manager can support it.

## LEV-4 Span and layers

**LEV-4.1** A line manager is expected to support up to ten direct reports. Beyond ten, the team is split and a new line manager is appointed.

**LEV-4.2** The company keeps its management chain shallow: no more than six layers from the CEO to an individual contributor.

## LEV-5 Leveling decisions

**LEV-5.1** Levels are set at hire from the level of the approved requisition, and change only through the promotion process in the Promotion and Calibration policy.

**LEV-5.2** A level is never lowered as a performance measure. Performance concerns are handled under the Performance Management policy.
